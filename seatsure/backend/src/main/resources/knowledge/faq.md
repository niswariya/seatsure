# SeatSure frequently asked questions

## Why did my seats disappear from the map?
The seat map updates live. When another person holds or books seats, they turn orange (on hold) or grey (sold) on your screen within a second. If someone's hold expires without payment, those seats become available again.

## Why does it say a seat was just taken?
Popular events sell fast. If two people try to hold the same seat at the same moment, only one of them gets it; the other sees "one or more of those seats was just taken". Pick different seats and try again; your card is never charged for a failed hold.

## Can I choose seats next to each other?
Yes. Select adjacent seats on the map, or ask the SeatSure assistant for "2 seats together" and it will suggest the best adjacent seats available.

## Is my payment secure?
Payments are processed by our payment partner over an encrypted connection. SeatSure never stores your card number.

## How do I contact support?
Email support@seatsure.dev. We reply within 24 hours, and within 2 hours on event days.
