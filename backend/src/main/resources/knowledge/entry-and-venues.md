# SeatSure entry rules and venue guide

## What to bring for entry
Show the QR code from your booking confirmation on your phone, or a printout. Carry a government photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN card, passport or driving licence); the name must match the account that booked. Gates open 60 minutes before the start time.

## Age limits
Children under 5 enter free if they sit on a parent's lap and don't need their own seat. Everyone aged 5 and above needs a ticket. Some concerts are 18+; this is shown on the event page, and ID is checked at the gate.

## Items not allowed
Outside food and drinks, professional cameras with detachable lenses, selfie sticks, umbrellas, power banks above 10,000 mAh, and any sharp objects are not allowed inside. Small bags are allowed and are security-checked at the gate.

## Marina Arena, Chennai
Marina Arena has 10 rows of seating, rows A to J. Rows A and B are Premium, rows C to G are Standard and rows H to J are Economy. Paid parking is available for 300 cars; arrive early on concert nights. Food stalls and drinking water are inside the arena.

## Lakeside Convention Hall, Bengaluru
Lakeside Convention Hall has 8 rows, A to H. Row A and B are Premium, rows C to E are Standard and rows F to H are Economy. The hall is air-conditioned. Free Wi-Fi is available, and a café is open during breaks.

## Accessibility
Both venues have step-free access, accessible washrooms and wheelchair spaces at the end of rows C and D. Wheelchair users and one companion can request these spaces by emailing support@seatsure.dev at least 48 hours before the event. Assistance dogs are welcome.
