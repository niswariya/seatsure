# SeatSure booking and refund policy

## How seat holds work
When you select seats and press "Hold seats", SeatSure reserves them for you for 5 minutes. Nobody else can book them during that time. A countdown shows how long you have left. If you don't pay before it reaches zero, the seats are released automatically and other people can book them. You can hold up to 6 seats per event at a time, and only one hold per event.

## Paying for your booking
Payment happens when you press "Pay and confirm" while your hold is active. If your connection drops or you press the button twice, you will not be charged twice: SeatSure recognises a repeated payment request and returns the same booking. Every confirmed booking gets a reference that starts with "SS-", for example SS-7KQ2M9XA.

## Cancellations and refunds
You can cancel a booking up to 48 hours before the event starts and get a full refund, minus a convenience fee of ₹30 per seat. Cancellations between 48 and 24 hours before the event get a 50% refund. Within 24 hours of the start time, bookings cannot be cancelled or refunded. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. To cancel, email support@seatsure.dev with your booking reference.

## If an event is cancelled or rescheduled
If the organiser cancels an event, every booking is refunded in full, including fees, automatically within 7 working days. If an event is rescheduled, your tickets stay valid for the new date. If you can't attend the new date, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of the announcement.

## Transferring tickets
Tickets are linked to the account that booked them. You can transfer a ticket to a friend once, up to 24 hours before the event, by emailing support with the booking reference and your friend's name and email address.
